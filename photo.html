1. Project Summary

I chose to restore an old photograph that had a rip in the center. I chose this option to show what I have learned about photo restoration.

2. Asset List:

I had just one asset. An old image of a man named Pirou Bertinot that I got from Category:Damaged photographs - Wikimedia Commons.

3. Explanation of the tools:

I started by opening the image in photoshop and changing the image to a Smart Object and added a new layer called Healing so I can make all the adjustments to the image, non-destructive. The image was a bit blurry, so I added a filter to sharpen the image. The final image has no longer a tear in the middle and looks a bit sharper.

4. Challenges

A challenge was trying to get the image colored. I struggled to get it to a different color to make it look more modern. But it wasn't going how I wanted, so I decided against that and just sharpened it and cleaned up the image.
