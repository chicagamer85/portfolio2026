Adobe Photoshop Project 1

Nicole Jensen

4/12/2026

1. Project Summary

I chose to do a Magazine Cover. The purpose of the project was to showcase what I’ve learned in photoshop training, specifically about layering and the magazine cover sounded fun to create.

2. Asset List

I took a downloadable image from Adobe Stock that feature a woman, I chose one with some style to her to go along with my Vintage magazine cover title. And I used some text and generative fill to fill in some gaps from the image.

3. Explanation of Tools

I first saved the image from Adobe stock and then in photoshop I created a new canvas sized 8.5x11px to make it vertical like a magazine. Then I started layering each asset, the first is the woman, she was a bit short for where I wanted it, so I used a layer of generative fill to fill in the gap, third layer is the title of magazine Vintage, used some additional layering to create bevel and emboss which gives it a bit of dimension and shadowing to make the title pop! Next was the layer of text for the Art of Beauty and changed the color, lastly the layer of master your mirror text. All to create a magazine that gives beauty tips to woman who want to master their mirror.

4. Challenges

Overall, this wasn’t a big challenge for me, I have some photoshop experience so overall this 1st project wasn’t too difficult. The hardest part was getting the idea out and onto the canvas.
