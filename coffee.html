Adobe Animate Project 3

Nicole Jensen

02/10/2026

1. Project Summary

I chose to do a product advertisement for a made-up coffee company. It is very simple and short. It features a coffee cup, a brown background and text and steam coming from the coffee cup.

2. Asset List:

- I took the coffee cup from Adobe Stock images, I tried to draw my own, but it proved to be difficult. I drew the steam and added the brown background and text.

3. Explanation of the tools:

- I started off by finding an image of a coffee cup, then in adobe animate I added layers for each instance, the background, coffee cup, steam and text. I first added the brown background on the stage and then added the coffee cup, and in frame 1 the coffee cup is zoomed out and it slowly zooms in with steam added and disappearing and then the text appears in the final frames.

4. Challenges

- The challenge I faced was designing this whole product advertisement, it was my very first time doing this and I struggled with adding the frames and getting the right movements, so it flowed. I did the best I could, I’m sure with more practice I will get better.
