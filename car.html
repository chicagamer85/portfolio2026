1. Project Summary

I chose to do a Social Media Ad Campaign and chose to do a car ad; it’s fitting for my full-time job as I work for a Car Dealership.

2. Asset List:

The car image was used from a free website called Freepik. That was my only saved asset; the rest of the AD is stuff I created in photoshop.

3. Explanation of the tools:

I added a layer for the Headline Luxury in every mile experience the drive you deserve. Another layer was added to the CTA button and added text of Start Your Journey. I needed to add some fill for the top of the image because I wanted to drop the image down and didn’t want white space. I added some styles to the text. And made my own logo (the company is made up), the mountain logo was created by Generative fill.

4. Challenges

This wasn’t a big challenge for me; I had fun creating my own social media ad. It’s simple and to the point and looks like Instagram worthy.
